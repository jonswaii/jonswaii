Why "Gong"

Named for The Gong.

Leonard Percival Howell, known as The Gong, was one of the first preachers of Rastafari. In 1940 he founded Pinnacle in Sligoville, St. Catherine, the first Rastafarian village in Jamaica. It was a self-sufficient community that lived off the land it worked.

True Gong is made by his grandson, in that same spirit: grown from the earth, pressed by hand, shared with the people.
